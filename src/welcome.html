YTGet

YouTube → MP3 or MP4, in two clicks.

Download buttons appear automatically next to the like/share row on every watch page. Or click the YTGet toolbar icon to grab any URL.

1
Pin YTGet to your toolbar
Click the puzzle icon in the browser toolbar, then click the pin next to YTGet.
2
Open any YouTube video
The MP3 / MP4 buttons appear right next to the Like and Share buttons. Click and the file lands in your Downloads/YTGet/ folder.
3
Or paste a URL into the popup
Click the YTGet icon, paste a link, and pick the quality you want — MP3 up to 320 kbps or MP4 up to 1080p.
